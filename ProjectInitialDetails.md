First Draft(Resume to Job Matcher)
1. Problem Statement and Target Users
What real-world problem does your application aim to solve? Who are the intended users of the application?
Ans: Our application aims to eliminate the hassle of job searching by helping job seekers find jobs that best suit their level of skill and experience. 

2. User Inputs 
What information or data will users provide to the system? 
Ans: Users will provide their resume to the system.

3. Use of AI 
How will AI be utilized within the application? What outputs, insights, or recommendations will the AI generate from the user inputs? 
Ans: AI will identify the user's skills and level of experience and compare them with online job listings. Then after, the AI will provide a compatibility score for users to see how well their resume matches against what the job market is looking for. The AI will also provide information about key areas the user is lacking in.

4. Business Rules 
What business rules, validations, or decision-making logic will be applied to the AI-generated outputs?
Ans: The AI will only extract important information from the user's resume such as the user's relevant industry/work experience, years of experience, necessary skills & qualifications and education/field of study. Using this information, the AI will compare and prioritise job listings where the user meets the key requirements and highlight areas that the user is lacking in. The AI will not take into account irrelevant information such as the user's gender and age.
